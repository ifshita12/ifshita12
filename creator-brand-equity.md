# Creator brand equity

An MBA research project by Ifshita on how a creator can turn an audience into a durable brand. It asks which parts of brand equity carry over from companies to individuals, and which assets a creator can retain when a platform changes.

The paper applies Aaker’s and Keller’s frameworks to four mechanisms, including owned distribution and product development. Four comparison tables connect the frameworks to creator decisions. The analysis is conceptual and uses published literature and illustrative cases. Its findings do not estimate the financial return of any individual creator.
